

## 英文口述稿｜自我介绍与工作经历


### 自我介绍｜Tell me about yourself

Hello, my name is Curtis Liu. I'm a software engineer with over eight years of experience in full-stack development, cloud systems, and AI applications.

At NTT, I built inventory services in C# and .NET for a global online store. My main job on that project was to keep stock levels in sync across regions. Each region had a local copy, so customers could check it quickly.  At checkout, we checked the main database before reserving an item. That helped us avoid selling more than we had.

I also built an AI agent for Databricks from start to finish. I used Python, RAG, LangGraph, and tool calling to help users search documents, generate SQL, and write code. The SQL tool supported about 3,500 tables.


### NTT｜2023年3月—2026年8月

One of my main projects at NTT in Japan was keeping stock levels in sync across regions. When stock changed in the main database, PostgreSQL copied the change to the other regions. That way, customers could see what was in stock without waiting for a reply from another region. We cut the P95 response time for overseas stock checks by about 85%.

A local copy could be a little behind. For example, two customers in different regions might both see that one item was left. At checkout, the inventory service checked both requests against the main database. Only one customer could reserve the last item.

In another project, I built an AI tool for Databricks to help users find answers, write code, and prepare plans. This role helped me understand both the customer's needs and the work needed to meet them.

### iHerb｜2021年8月—2023年2月

At iHerb in Shanghai, I worked on the checkout pages that customers used to place orders. I led the move to React and Next.js, and we cut the load time of our main pages by 76%.

I also built a tool for the team that managed product information. They could edit product descriptions and photos and see how they would look before publishing them.

### Databricks

Databricks is a cloud platform for data and AI. It helps teams process and analyze large amounts of data in one place. Teams can also use it to build AI models and agents with their business data. It also helps different teams work together using the same data.
